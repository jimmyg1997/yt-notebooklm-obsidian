## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Saving Challenges in Greece: The discussion highlights that saving money is particularly difficult due to low salaries and high living costs in Greece, but it is still feasible with proper planning.
- Discipline in Savings: The speakers stress the importance of setting aside at least 10% of one's income as savings to achieve financial freedom.
- Practical Strategies: Suggestions include establishing an automatic savings plan, utilizing high-yield savings accounts, and budgeting to track expenses and save more effectively.
- Earning Additional Income: The need for additional income through second jobs or freelance work is emphasized as a way to supplement earnings and bolster savings.
- Long-term Financial Growth: The importance of investing savings in high-yield accounts or assets that can generate passive income is discussed, highlighting that money should work for individuals instead of sitting idle.
- Save Automatically: Set up an automatic transfer of at least 10% of your income to a high-yield savings account to make saving easier and less tempting to spend.
- Consider Additional Income: Explore options for a second job or freelance work to increase your total income, allowing for more savings.
- Invest Wisely: Research and utilize high-yield savings accounts and consider investing in stocks or ETFs for passive income growth.
