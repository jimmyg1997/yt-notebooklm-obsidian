# ETFs and Index Funds

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Types of Investments: Investments aren't limited to stock markets; low-risk options like savings accounts or ETFs can also yield significant returns. Risks Involved: While investing carries inherent risks, having funds sit in a bank is also risky. Knowing the risk-to-reward ratio is crucial.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Types of Investments: Investments aren't limited to stock markets; low-risk options like savings accounts or ETFs can also yield significant returns.
- Risks Involved: While investing carries inherent risks, having funds sit in a bank is also risky. Knowing the risk-to-reward ratio is crucial.
- Fractional Ownership and Diversification: Investing in ETFs allows individuals to own fractions of various large companies, reducing specific stock risk.
- Consider ETFs: Use ETFs that track major indices, such as the S&P 500, to diversify without having to select individual stocks.
