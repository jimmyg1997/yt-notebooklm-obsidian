# Industrialization

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Low Industrial Contribution: Only 14% of Greece's GDP comes from the industrial sector, which is significantly low compared to other countries, highlighting a lack of industrialization. Need for Government Support: There is a clear need for effective government policies and support dedicated to developing the agricultural and industrial sectors rather than relying solely on tourism.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Low Industrial Contribution: Only 14% of Greece's GDP comes from the industrial sector, which is significantly low compared to other countries, highlighting a lack of industrialization.
- Need for Government Support: There is a clear need for effective government policies and support dedicated to developing the agricultural and industrial sectors rather than relying solely on tourism.
- Investment in Innovation: The presenters argue that investment in research and development (R&D) within industry can drive economic growth by producing higher-quality products and technologies.
- Explore opportunities for innovation in agriculture and industry through R&D investment.
