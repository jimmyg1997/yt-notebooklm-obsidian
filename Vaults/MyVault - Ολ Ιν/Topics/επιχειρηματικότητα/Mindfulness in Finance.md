# Mindfulness in Finance

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Evolving Financial Needs: As people age, their financial needs and costs typically rise, making it unrealistic to maintain the same lifestyle as in their younger years. Measurable Goals: Setting clear and measurable financial goals helps individuals achieve a stable and comfortable financial state, such as covering essential expenses through passive income.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Evolving Financial Needs: As people age, their financial needs and costs typically rise, making it unrealistic to maintain the same lifestyle as in their younger years.
- Measurable Goals: Setting clear and measurable financial goals helps individuals achieve a stable and comfortable financial state, such as covering essential expenses through passive income.
- Lifestyle Inflation: The tendency for lifestyle expectations to increase with income is discussed, advocating for financial discipline to avoid unnecessary spending as income rises.
- Financial Security Planning: Discussion of planning for financial security, including insurance and investment, to ensure stability for oneself and one's family.
- Gratitude and Self-Recognition: Encouraging self-acknowledgment and gratitude for financial achievements helps maintain motivation and perspective during the pursuit of financial goals.
- Strategic Allocation of Resources: The importance of planning resources—like investments, savings, and expenditures—carefully to enhance overall financial well-being.
- Set Clear Financial Goals: Define measurable financial targets or benchmarks, such as covering rent or bills through investments.
- Plan for Lifestyle Changes: Be mindful of the impact of lifestyle inflation and adjust spending habits accordingly as income changes.
