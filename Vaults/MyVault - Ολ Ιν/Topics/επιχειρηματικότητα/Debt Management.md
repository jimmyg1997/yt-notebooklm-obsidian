## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Financial Education: They note a lack of financial literacy among the population as a significant barrier to effective money management and argue for an increase in education around personal finance.
- Reduce Debt: Focus on managing and paying off debts, especially those with high interest rates, after establishing a financial cushion.
- Utilize Technology: Leverage apps and tools to track expenditures and investments easily, enhancing financial management.
- Actively reduce any high-interest debts and regularly review your monthly bills for possible savings.
- Debt Management: Not all debt is bad; strategic borrowing can fuel business growth while consumer debt tends to be detrimental.
- Educate on Debt: Learn to differentiate between good and bad debt to make informed financial decisions and avoid consumer pitfalls.
- Wealth Distribution Issues: The video points out that the past generations have left a legacy of debt for their children, particularly through inheritances tied to companies that suffered financial mismanagement.
