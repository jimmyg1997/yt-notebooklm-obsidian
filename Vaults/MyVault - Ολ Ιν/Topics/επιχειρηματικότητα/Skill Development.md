## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Skill Development: Building skills in negotiation, public speaking, and financial management can leverage personal and professional success.
- Maximizing Vacation Time: The hosts discuss how vacations can be used productively to catch up on tasks or enhance skills, paralleling weekends as periods that should not be wasted.
- Self-Investment: There is an emphasis on using free time for self-education and skill acquisition, especially during summer breaks when individuals have more flexibility.
- Plan Your Break: Utilize your vacation time to think about personal and professional development goals, not just relaxation.
- Invest in Learning: Consider enrolling in online courses or workshops during the summer to gain new skills or enhance existing ones.
- Continuous Learning: Always seek to improve your skills and knowledge by reading, attending courses, and learning from failures. This ongoing self-improvement builds the capacity to contribute value.
- Commit to continuous learning and self-improvement to enhance your skills.
- Skill Enhancement: Upgrading skills through online courses and training can lead to higher job offers or promotions within your current job.
