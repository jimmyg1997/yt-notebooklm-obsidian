# Cash Flow Management

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Cash Flow Management: The most significant risk to a business is not debt but cash flow issues. Entrepreneurs need to keep a close watch on cash flow projections over the next few years. Customer Relationship Management: It's essential to nurture relationships with existing customers. Retaining customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Cash Flow Management: The most significant risk to a business is not debt but cash flow issues. Entrepreneurs need to keep a close watch on cash flow projections over the next few years.
- Customer Relationship Management: It's essential to nurture relationships with existing customers. Retaining customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.
- Monitor cash flow continually and project future financial needs to avoid cash flow crises.
- Invest in customer relationship management to maintain loyalty and reduce churn.
