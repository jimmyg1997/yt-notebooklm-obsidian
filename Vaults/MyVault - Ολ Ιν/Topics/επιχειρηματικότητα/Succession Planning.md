# Succession Planning

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Mutual Growth: For a successful partnership, employees should be encouraged to take ownership of their roles, while employers need to share the company's successes and foster an environment where employees feel valued. Future-Proofing Workforce: Both employers and employees should be proactive in addressing the changing demands of the labor market to ensure long-term success.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Mutual Growth: For a successful partnership, employees should be encouraged to take ownership of their roles, while employers need to share the company's successes and foster an environment where employees feel valued.
- Future-Proofing Workforce: Both employers and employees should be proactive in addressing the changing demands of the labor market to ensure long-term success.
- Encourage Ownership: Empower employees by allowing them to take ownership of their work and contribute to the company's success.
