# Secondary Sector

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Low Industrial Contribution: Only 14% of Greece's GDP comes from the industrial sector, which is significantly low compared to other countries, highlighting a lack of industrialization. Importance of Primary Sector: The primary (agricultural) and secondary (manufacturing) sectors are crucial for producing new wealth and creating job opportunities in Greece.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Low Industrial Contribution: Only 14% of Greece's GDP comes from the industrial sector, which is significantly low compared to other countries, highlighting a lack of industrialization.
- Importance of Primary Sector: The primary (agricultural) and secondary (manufacturing) sectors are crucial for producing new wealth and creating job opportunities in Greece.
- Need for Government Support: There is a clear need for effective government policies and support dedicated to developing the agricultural and industrial sectors rather than relying solely on tourism.
- Understand that supporting the primary and secondary sectors is essential for Greece's long-term economic viability.
