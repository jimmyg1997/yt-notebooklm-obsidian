## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Wealth Distribution Issues: The video points out that the past generations have left a legacy of debt for their children, particularly through inheritances tied to companies that suffered financial mismanagement.
- Shifting Responsibility: The narrative suggests that individuals should not merely accept what was taught by previous generations but should question and learn from modern financial education.
- Educate Yourself on Investments: Consider learning about investing in stocks or other financial instruments to build wealth rather than just saving in a bank.
- Reframe Financial Goals: Adopt a mindset of continuous improvement in income and financial understanding, seeking to grow wealth.
- Share Financial Wisdom: Teach the next generation about modern financial strategies to avoid repeating the mistakes of the past.
