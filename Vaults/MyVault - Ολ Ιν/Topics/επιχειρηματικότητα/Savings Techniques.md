# Savings Techniques

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Importance of Savings: Many remain in financial struggle because they avoid saving or investing, fearing the discomfort of sacrificing immediate pleasures for long-term benefits. Begin saving a portion of your income, no matter how small, and invest it wisely.
