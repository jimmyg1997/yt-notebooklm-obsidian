# Saving Strategies for Parents

## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Long-Term Planning: Parents should think long-term about the costs involved in raising a child, including education and healthcare, and start saving early to prepare for future expenses.
- Investment for Future: The video suggests that regular savings and investments are crucial to build a financial foundation for a child, emphasizing that parents should plan for their child's future expenses from an early stage.
- Evaluate Financial Health: Before planning for a child, assess your financial stability and savings to ensure you can meet a child’s needs.
- Start Saving Early: Begin setting aside funds specifically for the future expenses of raising a child, including education.
