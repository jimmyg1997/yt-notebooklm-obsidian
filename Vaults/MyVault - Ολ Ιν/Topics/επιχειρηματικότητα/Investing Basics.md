# Investing Basics

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments. Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.
- Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
- Unnecessary Subscriptions: People often pay for premium services or subscriptions they don’t fully utilize. This drains resources that could be saved or invested.
- Investing for the Future: Long-term financial health relies on consistent savings and investments, regaining control over personal finances.
- Invest Regularly: Set aside a portion of your income for savings and investments each month to build a more secure financial future.
