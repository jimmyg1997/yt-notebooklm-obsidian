# Ethical Business Practices

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Value of a Strong Network: Building and maintaining a professional network is essential for success. It's important to leverage connections ethically, contrasting it with nepotism which involves unearned favoritism. Delivering Value: Successful individuals often go above and beyond in their roles, providing more value than expected. This not only enhances reputation but also encourages referrals and repeat business.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Value of a Strong Network: Building and maintaining a professional network is essential for success. It's important to leverage connections ethically, contrasting it with nepotism which involves unearned favoritism.
- Delivering Value: Successful individuals often go above and beyond in their roles, providing more value than expected. This not only enhances reputation but also encourages referrals and repeat business.
- Strategic Use of Resources: Seek out funding options, grants, or community programs to support entrepreneurial endeavors, but avoid becoming overly reliant on these to define one’s business approach.
- Learning from Others: Observe and adapt successful practices from others, acknowledging that copying successful strategies (without infringement) can pave the way to your achievements.
- Regularly evaluate and act upon funding opportunities that can help grow your business.
