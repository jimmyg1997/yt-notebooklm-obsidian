# Social Equity

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Impact on Social Equity: They discuss how the reliance on black money distorts public services and financial support for lower-income groups, worsening inequalities in society. Social Responsibility: There is a push for individuals and businesses to recognize the broader social implications of their financial choices, especially concerning tax compliance.
