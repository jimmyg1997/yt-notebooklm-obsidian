# Long-term Financial Planning

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness. Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness.
- Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it.
- Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
- Practice Patience: Understand that financial growth takes time, and do not panic during market fluctuations—stay committed to your long-term strategies.
- Early Retirement Possibility: The discussion revolves around the idea that individuals can set themselves up for retirement as young as in their 30s through strategic financial planning.
- Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE): The FIRE movement encourages extreme saving and investment strategies that allow individuals to retire much earlier than traditional ages.
- Personal Finance Management: The video emphasizes the necessity of managing finances effectively to achieve economic freedom, which is essential for those seeking to retire early.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> We must first change the way we view money before we can change our financial situation.
> If you are consistently in a state of financial distress, it is your choices that have led you there.
