# Employee Engagement

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Employee-Employer Tensions: There is a perceived opposition between employee and employer interests, which can create barriers to effective collaboration. Job Market Challenges: Many businesses struggle to find qualified candidates due to shifts in workforce availability and preferences, with many individuals pursuing freelance or entrepreneurial opportunities instead of traditional employment.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Employee-Employer Tensions: There is a perceived opposition between employee and employer interests, which can create barriers to effective collaboration.
- Job Market Challenges: Many businesses struggle to find qualified candidates due to shifts in workforce availability and preferences, with many individuals pursuing freelance or entrepreneurial opportunities instead of traditional employment.
- Importance of Cultural Fit: Employers should prioritize hiring individuals who align with the company's values and culture, rather than solely focusing on skills and experience.
- Mutual Growth: For a successful partnership, employees should be encouraged to take ownership of their roles, while employers need to share the company's successes and foster an environment where employees feel valued.
- Relationship Building: Establishing solid relationships through effective communication and mutual respect can lead to a more engaged and productive workforce.
- Flexibility in Hiring: Employers increasingly need to adopt flexible hiring strategies, such as project-based contracts, to attract talent in a competitive market.
- Future-Proofing Workforce: Both employers and employees should be proactive in addressing the changing demands of the labor market to ensure long-term success.
- Focus on Cultural Fit: Evaluate potential employees for their compatibility with the company culture and values during the interview process.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> The most significant risk for an employee is comfort; eventually, that comfort will unsettle you.
> Employers need to share profits and results; everyone must benefit.
