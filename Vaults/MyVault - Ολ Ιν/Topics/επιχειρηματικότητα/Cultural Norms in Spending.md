## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Mindset Shift: They advocate for a shift in mindset, encouraging individuals to live below their means and resist societal pressures for unnecessary spending.
- Breaking Social Taboos: The speakers call for a cultural change regarding living situations, suggesting embracing shared living arrangements to reduce costs.
- Budget and Track Expenses: Create a detailed budget to understand spending habits and identify unnecessary expenses that can be reduced or eliminated.
- Challenge Spending Norms: Work to break the cultural norm of keeping up with peers by prioritizing essential needs over wants.
