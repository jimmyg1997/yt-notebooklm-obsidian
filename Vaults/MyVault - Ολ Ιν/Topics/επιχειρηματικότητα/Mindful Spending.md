# Mindful Spending

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Mindful Spending: Being aware of the consequences of spending choices is essential. Spending money on non-essentials can limit future investments that yield higher returns. Value of Quality Services: Sometimes spending more on services (like cleaning) can free up time for more productive endeavors, thus being a better investment.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Mindful Spending: Being aware of the consequences of spending choices is essential. Spending money on non-essentials can limit future investments that yield higher returns.
- Value of Quality Services: Sometimes spending more on services (like cleaning) can free up time for more productive endeavors, thus being a better investment.
- Assess Spending Decisions: Regularly evaluate purchases against their opportunity costs to ensure you're not sacrificing more valuable options.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> The real cost of spending that €50 on a t-shirt is not just the money itself, but the future potential of what that money could have earned.
