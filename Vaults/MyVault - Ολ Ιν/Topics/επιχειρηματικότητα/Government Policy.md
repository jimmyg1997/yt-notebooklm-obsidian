# Government Policy

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Need for Government Support: There is a clear need for effective government policies and support dedicated to developing the agricultural and industrial sectors rather than relying solely on tourism. Challenges for Producers: Successful agricultural producers often still face high costs and inadequate support from the government, which hampers their ability to compete globally.
