# Economic Value vs. Perceived Value

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Non-productive vs. Productive Assets: Non-productive assets (like gold and certain cryptocurrencies) do not generate cash flow or societal value. In contrast, productive assets (like rental properties or stocks) create wealth and provide services or goods that benefit society. Illusion of Gold’s Value: The discussion critiques the belief that gold holds intrinsic value, highlighting that its price is largely driven by social contract rather than economic productivity.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Non-productive vs. Productive Assets: Non-productive assets (like gold and certain cryptocurrencies) do not generate cash flow or societal value. In contrast, productive assets (like rental properties or stocks) create wealth and provide services or goods that benefit society.
- Illusion of Gold’s Value: The discussion critiques the belief that gold holds intrinsic value, highlighting that its price is largely driven by social contract rather than economic productivity.
- Evaluate Investments: Before making an investment, critically assess whether it is a productive asset that generates income or a non-productive one without intrinsic value.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> Gold has no intrinsic value; it’s merely a social contract that gives it perceived value.
