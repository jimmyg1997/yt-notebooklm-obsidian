# FIRE Movement

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE): The FIRE movement encourages extreme saving and investment strategies that allow individuals to retire much earlier than traditional ages. Different Paths to FIRE: Several approaches are discussed, such as frugality, job hopping for higher wages, and balancing living expenses while saving.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE): The FIRE movement encourages extreme saving and investment strategies that allow individuals to retire much earlier than traditional ages.
- Different Paths to FIRE: Several approaches are discussed, such as frugality, job hopping for higher wages, and balancing living expenses while saving.
- Pragmatic Approaches to Spending: Strategies like “Barista FIRE” are mentioned, which entail maintaining a low-cost lifestyle while working in a job that finances living expenses without requiring a significant time commitment.
