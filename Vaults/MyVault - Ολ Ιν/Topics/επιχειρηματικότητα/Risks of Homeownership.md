# Risks of Homeownership

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Misguided Beliefs: Many individuals believe that real estate is a stable investment without considering market fluctuations or the risks involved, such as economic downturns. Educate Yourself: Increase financial literacy to make informed decisions, understanding the risks associated with real estate and other investments.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Misguided Beliefs: Many individuals believe that real estate is a stable investment without considering market fluctuations or the risks involved, such as economic downturns.
- Educate Yourself: Increase financial literacy to make informed decisions, understanding the risks associated with real estate and other investments.
- Evaluate Personal Goals: Assess whether homeownership aligns with long-term financial goals or if renting might provide better flexibility and security.
