# Toxic Relationships

## Σύντομη εικόνα

The Role of Family: The speakers touch on familial relationships, stressing the importance of maintaining boundaries to prevent negative influences from close relatives from affecting personal well-being. Self-Responsibility: They advocate for taking responsibility for one’s emotional well-being and being proactive in distancing oneself from toxic influences.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- The Role of Family: The speakers touch on familial relationships, stressing the importance of maintaining boundaries to prevent negative influences from close relatives from affecting personal well-being.
- Self-Responsibility: They advocate for taking responsibility for one’s emotional well-being and being proactive in distancing oneself from toxic influences.
- Encourage Open Discussions: If surrounded by negativity, initiate discussions about feelings and experiences to address toxicity and seek improvement in interactions.
