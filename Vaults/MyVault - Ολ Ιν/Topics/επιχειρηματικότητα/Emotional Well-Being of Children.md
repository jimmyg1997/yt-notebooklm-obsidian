# Emotional Well-Being of Children

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Impact of External Pressures: Societal expectations and family pressures can lead individuals to have children before they are ready, further complicating their economic situation. Importance of Experiences: Couples are encouraged to invest time in their relationship and experiences together before having children, which can create a stronger foundation for family life.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Impact of External Pressures: Societal expectations and family pressures can lead individuals to have children before they are ready, further complicating their economic situation.
- Importance of Experiences: Couples are encouraged to invest time in their relationship and experiences together before having children, which can create a stronger foundation for family life.
- Child's Well-Being: The emotional and psychological welfare of a child should be prioritized over economic limitations, ensuring that children do not suffer due to financial instability.
- Limit External Pressures: Be resilient against societal and familial expectations regarding timing and having children, and make decisions that are right for you.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> A child is a blessing, but that blessing should not come at the cost of their well-being due to financial strain.
