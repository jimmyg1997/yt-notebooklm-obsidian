## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Embrace Change Management: The hosts stress that adapting to change is essential for any growing business. They note that introducing new systems often faces resistance due to human nature and routine.
- Cultivate Change Management Skills: Invest in training for yourself and your team to better manage and implement changes within your organization.
- Innovation Resistance: The speakers mention the tendency among Greeks to criticize new ideas or changes, ultimately hindering innovation and progress.
- Generational Shift: There's hope in observing changes in Gen Z attitudes, with a growing entrepreneurial spirit and willingness to embrace new opportunities, unlike previous generations.
- Practice Optimism: Cultivate a positive outlook by recognizing and embracing opportunities for growth and change, rather than fearing the unknown.
- Foster Generational Change: Encourage younger generations to embrace entrepreneurial opportunities and innovation rather than succumbing to the despair of past experiences.
