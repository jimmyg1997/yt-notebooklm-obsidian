# Entrepreneurial Mindset

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it. Shift Mindset: Work on changing your perception of money to see it as a tool to enhance your freedom and opportunities.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it.
- Shift Mindset: Work on changing your perception of money to see it as a tool to enhance your freedom and opportunities.
- Mindset Shift: View yourself as a business and embrace an entrepreneurial approach to your career, continuously seeking improvement and value addition.
- Mindset is Key: Success starts with a mindset shift; individuals must believe they can improve their circumstances instead of feeling trapped by their current situation.
- Assess and change your current mindset about money and financial success.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> It’s easier to earn money than to retain it if you don't change your mindset first.
> You are the entrepreneur of yourself and should constantly seek to upgrade your skills and value.
