# Child-Rearing Responsibilities

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Societal Pressures: There are societal norms that pressure individuals, especially women, to marry and have children by certain ages, leading to rushed decisions that may not align with personal circumstances. Impact of Children: The impacts of having children and the responsibilities that come with parenthood are highlighted, pointing out that individuals should consider their readiness, both emotionally and financially, before starting a family.
