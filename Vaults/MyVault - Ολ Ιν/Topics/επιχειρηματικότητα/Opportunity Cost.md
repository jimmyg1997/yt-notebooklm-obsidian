# Opportunity Cost

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Opportunity Cost: Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative foregone when making a choice, and it isn't solely financial; it includes time and personal well-being. Investing in Future Self: Allocating a percentage of income towards investments is crucial for financial growth and security; neglecting this can lead to missed opportunities for wealth accumulation.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Opportunity Cost: Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative foregone when making a choice, and it isn't solely financial; it includes time and personal well-being.
- Investing in Future Self: Allocating a percentage of income towards investments is crucial for financial growth and security; neglecting this can lead to missed opportunities for wealth accumulation.
- Assess Spending Decisions: Regularly evaluate purchases against their opportunity costs to ensure you're not sacrificing more valuable options.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> The real cost of spending that €50 on a t-shirt is not just the money itself, but the future potential of what that money could have earned.
> “Investing in your future self should be a priority; neglecting it can lead to missed opportunities for financial security.”
