# Business Model Innovation

## Σύντομη εικόνα

## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Identify Your Idea: Begin with a clear business idea and evaluate its market value through competitive analysis. Understanding the problem you're solving is crucial.
- Business Plan: Develop a business and marketing plan that outlines your goals and strategies but keep it flexible to adapt to changing market conditions.
- Find a Good Accountant: Establish a relationship with a reliable accountant who can help you navigate the financial and regulatory aspects of your business.
- Sales Focus: Shift your mindset from being just a service provider to actively seeking and securing customers. Sales are the lifeblood of your business.
- Develop a business plan that is adaptable to change.
