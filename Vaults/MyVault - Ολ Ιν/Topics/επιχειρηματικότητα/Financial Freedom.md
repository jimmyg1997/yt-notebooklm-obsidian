# Financial Freedom

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness. Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness.
- Mindset Shift: Success and financial independence stem from a change in mindset regarding money—first, the way we perceive money, and then the way we handle it.
- Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification.
- Shift Mindset: Work on changing your perception of money to see it as a tool to enhance your freedom and opportunities.
- Practice Patience: Understand that financial growth takes time, and do not panic during market fluctuations—stay committed to your long-term strategies.
- Discipline in Savings: The speakers stress the importance of setting aside at least 10% of one's income as savings to achieve financial freedom.
- Long-term Financial Growth: The importance of investing savings in high-yield accounts or assets that can generate passive income is discussed, highlighting that money should work for individuals instead of sitting idle.
- Financial Education: They note a lack of financial literacy among the population as a significant barrier to effective money management and argue for an increase in education around personal finance.
