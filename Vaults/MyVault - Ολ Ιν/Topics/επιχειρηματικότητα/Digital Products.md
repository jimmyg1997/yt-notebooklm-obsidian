## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Dropshipping: Create an online store that connects customers to producers without holding inventory, allowing you to operate with minimal upfront costs.
- Affiliate Marketing: Promote products or services for a commission by creating content that directs people to purchase through unique links.
- Digital Products: Develop e-books, online courses, or other digital content to sell and earn passive income once created.
- Create Digital Products: Begin developing digital products that align with your expertise or interests, and consider platforms to market them.
- Content Creation: The speakers emphasize that creating content is a legitimate way to earn money online. Anyone can start producing videos, blogs, or podcasts using just their smartphone and personal interests.
- Monetization Methods: There are multiple paths to monetization, including ad revenue from platforms like YouTube, affiliate marketing, sponsorships, Patreon, and selling digital products.
- Affiliate Marketing: The video clarifies the difference between legitimate affiliate marketing (earning a commission by recommending products) and pyramid schemes, advocating for ethical practices.
- Digital Products: Developing and selling digital products such as e-books or planners can be a lucrative income stream. These products often require initial time investment but can generate passive income.
