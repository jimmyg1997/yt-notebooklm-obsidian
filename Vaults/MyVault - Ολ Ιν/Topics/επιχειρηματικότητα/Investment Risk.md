# Investment Risk

## Σύντομη εικόνα

## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Definition of Investment: Investment is the practice of using money to generate greater returns instead of mere consumption. It's a fundamental concept everyone should understand.
- Types of Investments: Investments aren't limited to stock markets; low-risk options like savings accounts or ETFs can also yield significant returns.
- Importance of Education: Understanding investments requires a certain level of education, as misinformed decisions can be precarious.
- Long-term Perspective: The hosts advocate for a long-term investment strategy, emphasizing the power of compound interest which can significantly increase wealth over decades.
- Risks Involved: While investing carries inherent risks, having funds sit in a bank is also risky. Knowing the risk-to-reward ratio is crucial.
- Comparison with Day Trading: Day trading is portrayed as a form of gambling rather than a reliable investment strategy. Consistent, long-term investing is recommended instead.
- Fractional Ownership and Diversification: Investing in ETFs allows individuals to own fractions of various large companies, reducing specific stock risk.
- Avoiding Market Manipulation: Ethical considerations and legalities, like insider trading, are crucial topics, as information asymmetry can unjustly favor certain investors.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> It is important to invest rather than simply keep money in savings, as it doesn't help you grow.
