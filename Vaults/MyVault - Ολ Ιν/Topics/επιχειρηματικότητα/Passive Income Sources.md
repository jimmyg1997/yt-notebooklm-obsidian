# Passive Income Sources

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness. Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness.
- Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
