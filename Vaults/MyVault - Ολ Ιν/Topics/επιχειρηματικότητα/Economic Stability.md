## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Economic Stability: The speakers stress that financial readiness is critical before entering marriage. A lack of financial stability can strain relationships, making it essential to have a solid economic background.
- Long-term Implications: The long-term consequences of financial decisions made during the early stages of marriage can significantly impact future opportunities for growth and stability within a family setup.
- Evaluate Financial Status: Ensure financial stability before considering marriage or starting a family—create a budget and savings plan to support long-term goals.
- Growth Mindset: The hosts advocate for a mentality of continually seeking to increase income and understanding of financial markets instead of settling for stability.
- Challenge Received Wisdom: Analyze financial advice from parents critically, recognizing that their experiences may not apply to current economic conditions.
