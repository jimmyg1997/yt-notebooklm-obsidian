# Startup Validation

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Validating Ideas Early: Entrepreneurs should seek to validate their business concepts by finding initial customers before investing heavily in formalizing their business structure. Take Action: Start small by seeking your first few customers to validate your business idea before worrying about the formalities of starting a company.
