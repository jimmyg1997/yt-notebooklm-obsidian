# Wealth Management

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments. Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
- Perception of Money: The notion that money equates to time, and emphasizing the need to view it as a tool for building wealth rather than just a means of immediate consumption.
- Wealth Distribution Issues: The video points out that the past generations have left a legacy of debt for their children, particularly through inheritances tied to companies that suffered financial mismanagement.
- Educate Yourself on Investments: Consider learning about investing in stocks or other financial instruments to build wealth rather than just saving in a bank.
- Reframe Financial Goals: Adopt a mindset of continuous improvement in income and financial understanding, seeking to grow wealth.
- Mindset Matters: Wealth is tied to an individual’s mindset and financial management skills rather than just accumulation of money.
- Wealth as a Reflection: Money magnifies who you are as a person—good or bad traits can be amplified with wealth, thus character matters more than financial status.
