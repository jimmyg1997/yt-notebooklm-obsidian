# E-commerce Strategies

## Σύντομη εικόνα

E-commerce Basics: Understanding the logistics of sourcing and customer service is crucial. It’s essential to be clear about what products can be sold and how they are delivered, including handling returns and customer dissatisfaction. High Competition: The current e-commerce market is saturated, and success often relies on niche markets or unique offerings rather than generic products.
