# Inflation Risk

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Inflation as a Hidden Tax: The hosts argue that inflation erodes purchasing power, making it crucial to invest rather than save money in low-interest accounts. Misconception of Saving: Merely saving money can be dangerous; it can expose one to inflation risks, while investment builds wealth over time.
