# Minimum Viable Product

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Minimum Viable Product (MVP): Create a minimum viable product to test your idea in the market, gather customer feedback, and refine your offering based on this input. Continuous Improvement: Always seek feedback and be willing to adapt and improve your product or service. Staying dynamic is key to longevity.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Minimum Viable Product (MVP): Create a minimum viable product to test your idea in the market, gather customer feedback, and refine your offering based on this input.
- Continuous Improvement: Always seek feedback and be willing to adapt and improve your product or service. Staying dynamic is key to longevity.
- Create a minimum viable product to test and refine your concept based on real customer feedback.
- Establish a strong online presence to effectively market and sell your product or service.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> When someone tells me they have an idea but don’t know the next step, my answer is to create the minimum viable product.
> The biggest mistake new entrepreneurs make is focusing too much on the product and not enough on how to actually sell it.
