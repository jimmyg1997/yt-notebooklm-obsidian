# Smart Spending

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Establish a Budget: Track your income and expenses meticulously to understand where your money goes and make informed decisions about your spending. Set Financial Goals: Create specific, measurable financial objectives to guide your spending and saving habits, such as saving for a vacation or an investment.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Establish a Budget: Track your income and expenses meticulously to understand where your money goes and make informed decisions about your spending.
- Set Financial Goals: Create specific, measurable financial objectives to guide your spending and saving habits, such as saving for a vacation or an investment.
- Identify and categorize your discretionary spending to determine areas for savings.
