# Self-Improvement Techniques

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Self-Assessment: Understand and improve your strengths and weaknesses, leveraging feedback from colleagues to enhance your professional profile. Mindset Shift: View yourself as a business and embrace an entrepreneurial approach to your career, continuously seeking improvement and value addition.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Self-Assessment: Understand and improve your strengths and weaknesses, leveraging feedback from colleagues to enhance your professional profile.
- Mindset Shift: View yourself as a business and embrace an entrepreneurial approach to your career, continuously seeking improvement and value addition.
- Proactive Communication: Don’t wait for opportunities or raises to come to you. Advocate for yourself with documented achievements when discussing promotions or salary increases.
- Continuous Learning: Stay updated with industry trends and skills to remain competitive in the job market; reading and self-education should continue beyond formal education.
- Regularly ask for feedback from colleagues and superiors to identify areas for improvement and develop a clear personal brand.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> You are the entrepreneur of yourself and should constantly seek to upgrade your skills and value.
