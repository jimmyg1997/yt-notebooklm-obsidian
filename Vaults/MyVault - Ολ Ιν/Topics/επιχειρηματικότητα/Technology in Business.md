## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Embrace Change Management: The hosts stress that adapting to change is essential for any growing business. They note that introducing new systems often faces resistance due to human nature and routine.
- The Importance of Technology: Incorporating technology into business processes can improve efficiency and outcomes. Emphasizing its benefits helps staff accept new tools.
- Streamlined Operations with Fewer Partners: Collaborating with fewer but more reliable partners can enhance business efficiency and profitability.
- Leverage Technology: Identify areas where technology can improve your operations and facilitate training programs for staff to ease the transition.
- Cash Flow Management: The most significant risk to a business is not debt but cash flow issues. Entrepreneurs need to keep a close watch on cash flow projections over the next few years.
- Sales Focus: Businesses must continually acquire new customers to sustain income. If the focus is solely on operational tasks without generating sales, they risk failure.
- Embracing Technology: Ignoring technological advancements can hinder growth. Entrepreneurs should leverage technology and automation to streamline operations.
- Ongoing Training and Development: Both entrepreneurs and employees should engage in continuous learning to adapt to market changes and improve the business.
