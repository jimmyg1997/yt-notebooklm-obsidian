# Immediate Gratification

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification. Immediate Gratification Trap: Many individuals prioritize immediate pleasures, such as vacations or luxury items, which leads to deeper financial struggles instead of focusing on long-term financial health.
