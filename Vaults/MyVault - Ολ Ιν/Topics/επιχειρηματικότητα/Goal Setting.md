## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Goal Reflection: It is important to analyze what has been achieved over the past year, understand personal financial conditions, and set practical goals for the upcoming period.
- Long-term Planning: Setting three-year goals rather than just focusing on annual targets enables individuals to have a bigger picture outlook and adapt their strategies accordingly.
- Plan Your Break: Utilize your vacation time to think about personal and professional development goals, not just relaxation.
- Set Clear Goals: Reflect on your past year's achievements and define specific actionable goals for the coming months.
- Regular Self-Assessment: Commit to periodic self-assessment to track progress towards goals and adjust your plans as necessary.
- Strategic Planning: The video suggests that the summer months are ideal for reflecting on goals and developing strategies for the next year, free from the daily pressures of work.
- Schedule Learning Goals: Decide on at least one new skill or subject to learn this summer and set aside time for it.
- Strategize Your Career: Use the quieter summer months to reflect on your career path and set long-term goals.
