# Expense Tracking

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Establish a Budget: Track your income and expenses meticulously to understand where your money goes and make informed decisions about your spending. Identify Unnecessary Expenses: Look for areas where you can cut back, particularly on smaller, frequent purchases that add up over time.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Establish a Budget: Track your income and expenses meticulously to understand where your money goes and make informed decisions about your spending.
- Identify Unnecessary Expenses: Look for areas where you can cut back, particularly on smaller, frequent purchases that add up over time.
- Create an Emergency Fund: Maintain savings equivalent to 3-6 months of living expenses to ensure financial stability during unexpected events.
- Start by creating a detailed budget that captures all income and expenses.
- Build and maintain an emergency fund that can cover 3-6 months of living expenses.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> If you don't track your expenses, you may not realize how quickly small purchases can add up.
