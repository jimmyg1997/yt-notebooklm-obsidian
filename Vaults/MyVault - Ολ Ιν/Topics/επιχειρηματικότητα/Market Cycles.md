# Market Cycles

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Active Market Cycles: The economy operates on cycles of growth and contraction, and investors should be prepared rather than trying to time the market based on fear. Understand Market Cycles: Recognize that economic cycles are natural, and resist the urge to react impulsively to market fluctuations.
