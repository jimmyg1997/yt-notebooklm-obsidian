## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Building a Strong Online Presence: The video highlights how investing in social media and content creation can significantly boost brand visibility and customer engagement.
- Focus on Customer Retention: Maintaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. They advocate for personalized service and regular check-ins with customers.
- Enhance Online Engagement: Commit to developing a strong online presence through content creation and active social media engagement to attract and retain customers.
- Prioritize Customer Relationships: Establish regular communication with clients to foster long-term relationships and improve retention rates.
- Sales Focus: Businesses must continually acquire new customers to sustain income. If the focus is solely on operational tasks without generating sales, they risk failure.
- Effective Marketing: Many entrepreneurs overlook the importance of marketing. Utilizing digital platforms and social media is crucial for reaching potential customers effectively.
- Customer Relationship Management: It's essential to nurture relationships with existing customers. Retaining customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.
- Collaborative Team Environment: Building a supportive and collaborative company culture can enhance employee satisfaction and retention.
