# Networking for Entrepreneurs

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Networking: Attend industry events and engage with your network to expand your customer base and credibility. Network continuously to secure customer relationships and potential sales opportunities.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Networking: Attend industry events and engage with your network to expand your customer base and credibility.
- Network continuously to secure customer relationships and potential sales opportunities.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> The biggest mistake new entrepreneurs make is focusing too much on the product and not enough on how to actually sell it.
> Most people want to play the role of an entrepreneur but aren't willing to do what it takes to be one.
