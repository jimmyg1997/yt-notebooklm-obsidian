# Long-term Planning

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification. Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Long-term Planning: Financial freedom takes patience and long-term planning, with commitment to consistent savings or investments rather than seeking immediate gratification.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
- Practice Patience: Understand that financial growth takes time, and do not panic during market fluctuations—stay committed to your long-term strategies.
- Long-term Planning: Setting three-year goals rather than just focusing on annual targets enables individuals to have a bigger picture outlook and adapt their strategies accordingly.
- Long-Term Planning: Parents should think long-term about the costs involved in raising a child, including education and healthcare, and start saving early to prepare for future expenses.
- Evaluate Financial Health: Before planning for a child, assess your financial stability and savings to ensure you can meet a child’s needs.
- Communicate with Partner: Discuss goals, expectations, and finances openly with your partner to create a united approach towards family planning.
- Importance of Savings: Many remain in financial struggle because they avoid saving or investing, fearing the discomfort of sacrificing immediate pleasures for long-term benefits.
