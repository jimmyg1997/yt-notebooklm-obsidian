# Workplace Culture

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Importance of Cultural Fit: Employers should prioritize hiring individuals who align with the company's values and culture, rather than solely focusing on skills and experience. Focus on Cultural Fit: Evaluate potential employees for their compatibility with the company culture and values during the interview process.
