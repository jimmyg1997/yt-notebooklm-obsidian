## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Economic Stability: The speakers stress that financial readiness is critical before entering marriage. A lack of financial stability can strain relationships, making it essential to have a solid economic background.
- Individual Choices: The discussion emphasizes that each person's journey is unique, and there is no one-size-fits-all timeline for marriage and family. Personal readiness can differ greatly between individuals.
- Impact of Children: The impacts of having children and the responsibilities that come with parenthood are highlighted, pointing out that individuals should consider their readiness, both emotionally and financially, before starting a family.
- Communicate with Partners: Have clear conversations with potential partners about financial readiness, life goals, and plans for family to ensure alignment.
- Reject Societal Pressures: Make decisions based on personal desires rather than societal expectations; recognize the importance of timing and personal readiness in life choices.
- Consider Future Impact: Reflect on how decisions related to marriage and family impact long-term financial and emotional well-being.
- Pursue Personal Growth: Focus on building a career or personal development as a means to enhance overall readiness for partnership and family life.
