# SMART Goals

## Σύντομη εικόνα

SMART Goals: Setting specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals is essential. Many individuals chase vague aspirations instead of structured objectives. Long-Term Perspective: Viewing decisions through a long-term lens rather than focusing on immediate goals can ease anxiety and foster better choices.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- SMART Goals: Setting specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals is essential. Many individuals chase vague aspirations instead of structured objectives.
- Long-Term Perspective: Viewing decisions through a long-term lens rather than focusing on immediate goals can ease anxiety and foster better choices.
- Set SMART Goals: Write down specific goals that are measurable and have deadlines to keep track of progress.
