# Time Management

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness. Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Time vs. Money: Time is a finite resource that cannot be purchased, and one's approach to managing time directly impacts their financial success and happiness.
- Earning vs. Retaining Wealth: It is often easier to earn money than to keep it, as many people do not learn to manage their wealth effectively, often relying on active income rather than investments.
- Value Time: Prioritize how you spend your time, focusing on activities that lead to personal growth and meaningful relationships.
- Invest for the Future: Consistently invest a portion of your income, even a small percentage, and let it grow over time to build long-term wealth.
- Practice Patience: Understand that financial growth takes time, and do not panic during market fluctuations—stay committed to your long-term strategies.
- Maximizing Vacation Time: The hosts discuss how vacations can be used productively to catch up on tasks or enhance skills, paralleling weekends as periods that should not be wasted.
- Self-Investment: There is an emphasis on using free time for self-education and skill acquisition, especially during summer breaks when individuals have more flexibility.
- Networking: Making connections during vacation time is presented as a valuable method to further personal and professional growth, suggesting informal gatherings are potential avenues for creating opportunities.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> Time is the only resource that is spent but never bought.
> The summer is a good time to reflect on the past year and prepare for the next, to take the time needed for personal growth and development.
> Investing in yourself during your downtime is not only productive but essential for your long-term success.
