## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Invest Wisely: Consider prudent investment strategies, utilizing platforms like Freedom 24 for managing finances and investments effectively.
- Health Insurance and Security: Investing in health and financial security at a young age is often overlooked but crucial for long-term stability. It mitigates risks associated with unforeseen challenges.
- Invest in Financial Education: Start learning about personal finance and investment strategies to make better financial decisions early on.
- Embrace Risks: Be willing to take calculated risks, especially in career choices and investments.
- Self-Investment: There is an emphasis on using free time for self-education and skill acquisition, especially during summer breaks when individuals have more flexibility.
- Long-term Planning: Setting three-year goals rather than just focusing on annual targets enables individuals to have a bigger picture outlook and adapt their strategies accordingly.
- Taking Practical Action: Practical steps, such as starting an investment account, learning online, or seeking courses, are encouraged as a way to start improving one’s financial situation.
- Invest in Learning: Consider enrolling in online courses or workshops during the summer to gain new skills or enhance existing ones.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις

> Investing in yourself during your downtime is not only productive but essential for your long-term success.
> The earlier you start investing, the more time you have for your money to grow.
> All non-productive assets are gambling while productive assets can be genuine investments.
