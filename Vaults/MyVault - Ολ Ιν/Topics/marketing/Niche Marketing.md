# Niche Marketing

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Affiliate Marketing as a Gateway: Starting with affiliate marketing can be a great way to learn about selling products online without upfront investment or needing a business license, allowing individuals to gauge market demand before scaling into dropshipping. High Competition: The current e-commerce market is saturated, and success often relies on niche markets or unique offerings rather than generic products.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Affiliate Marketing as a Gateway: Starting with affiliate marketing can be a great way to learn about selling products online without upfront investment or needing a business license, allowing individuals to gauge market demand before scaling into dropshipping.
- High Competition: The current e-commerce market is saturated, and success often relies on niche markets or unique offerings rather than generic products.
- Explore affiliate marketing as a low-risk entry into online selling to test product ideas before committing to dropshipping.
- Stay informed about the competitive landscape and adjust product offerings to cater to unique niches as opposed to mass-market items.
