- Affiliate Marketing: Promote products or services for a commission by creating content that directs people to purchase through unique links.
- Engage Online: Start a social media presence for content creation or as an affiliate marketer to generate income.
- Create Digital Products: Begin developing digital products that align with your expertise or interests, and consider platforms to market them.
- Monetization Methods: There are multiple paths to monetization, including ad revenue from platforms like YouTube, affiliate marketing, sponsorships, Patreon, and selling digital products.
- Affiliate Marketing: The video clarifies the difference between legitimate affiliate marketing (earning a commission by recommending products) and pyramid schemes, advocating for ethical practices.

- Explore Affiliate Marketing: Investigate reputable affiliate programs related to your niche and consider promoting products that align with your content.
