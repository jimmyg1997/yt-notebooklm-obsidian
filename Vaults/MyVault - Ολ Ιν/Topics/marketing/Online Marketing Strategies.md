## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Identify Your Idea: Begin with a clear business idea and evaluate its market value through competitive analysis. Understanding the problem you're solving is crucial.
- Minimum Viable Product (MVP): Create a minimum viable product to test your idea in the market, gather customer feedback, and refine your offering based on this input.
- Business Plan: Develop a business and marketing plan that outlines your goals and strategies but keep it flexible to adapt to changing market conditions.
- Online Presence: Build your brand's online visibility. This step is essential in today’s digital world to attract customers and drive sales.
- Start with a clear idea and research its value in the market.
- Establish a strong online presence to effectively market and sell your product or service.
