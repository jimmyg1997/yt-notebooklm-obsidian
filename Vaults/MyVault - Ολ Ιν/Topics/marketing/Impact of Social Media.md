# Impact of Social Media

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Impact of Social Media Engagement: Negative comments, paradoxically, can boost visibility for creators due to engagement metrics. However, creators are encouraged to prioritize positive interactions that contribute to a healthier community. Personal Responsibility: Both creators and commenters have a responsibility to reflect on what they say and how it impacts others. This awareness can foster a more constructive online environment.


## Τι λένε τα βίντεο

- Impact of Social Media Engagement: Negative comments, paradoxically, can boost visibility for creators due to engagement metrics. However, creators are encouraged to prioritize positive interactions that contribute to a healthier community.
- Personal Responsibility: Both creators and commenters have a responsibility to reflect on what they say and how it impacts others. This awareness can foster a more constructive online environment.
- Prioritize Positive Impact: Before posting comments or creating content, evaluate whether it brings value to the conversation.


## Σημαντικές παραθέσεις
