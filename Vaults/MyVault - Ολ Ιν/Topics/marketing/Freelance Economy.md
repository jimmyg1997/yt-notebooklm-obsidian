# Freelance Economy

## Σύντομη εικόνα

Job Market Challenges: Many businesses struggle to find qualified candidates due to shifts in workforce availability and preferences, with many individuals pursuing freelance or entrepreneurial opportunities instead of traditional employment. Adopt Flexible Contracts: Consider offering project-based or freelance contracts to attract skilled candidates who prefer flexibility over traditional employment.
